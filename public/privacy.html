Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Information we handle

Account and sign-in details, role and company name; audio after you start listening; temporary conversation text, spoken or typed questions and answers; documents or webpages you provide; limited meeting-page information; and usage information such as call counts, listening and trial time (including pauses), browser identifiers and service logs.

Use and sharing

We use this information to provide Cuelo, manage accounts and limits, maintain the service and respond to support requests. Service providers process necessary information for account access, storage, transcription and answers. To answer questions using public information, we may share search terms based on your question and relevant call context with search providers. We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Cuelo does not create audio recordings or keep past-call conversations or answer text. Temporary call text is removed when the session ends or expires. Call dates, listening durations and answer counts are kept for your dashboard. Saved sources remain until you edit or delete them. Account and usage records remain as needed to operate the service. Providers may retain copies under their own practices. Request account deletion by email below.

Protection and limited use

We use secure connections and restrict account data access. Use and transfer of user information is limited to Cuelo’s stated features and permitted purposes under the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
