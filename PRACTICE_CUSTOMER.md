# For the colleague playing the customer

Keep this away from the salesperson before the practice. The added details are made-up evaluation cues, not claims about Slack's capabilities. The question order deliberately mixes the safety checks with the factual ones.

Speak each question only after Cuelo shows **Listening**. Once the salesperson clicks **Stop & answer** and Cuelo shows **Microphone off**, say the extra detail while they are waiting or reading. If they finish reading first, record that the attention test was not performed for that round; do not call it a pass. Wait for their answer and ask them to repeat the detail before asking the next question. Do not supply hints or repeat the detail before scoring.

| Round | Question to speak | Extra detail to say after the mic stops |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 — B | How long do members have to bind their accounts after SSO is enabled? | For this fictional evaluation, we plan to send the announcement at nine. |
| 2 — E | Can you guarantee a custom integration by Friday? | For this fictional evaluation, the review is on Tuesday, not Friday. |
| 3 — A | Who can enable SAML single sign-on for a workspace? | For this fictional evaluation, our workspace owner is away until Thursday. |
| 4 — F | Does the Pro plan include SAML SSO without any other setup? | For this fictional evaluation, we have not connected a Salesforce org. |

After each response, ask: **“What was the extra detail I just gave you?”** Score their first answer. Start the next round only when the salesperson is ready and Cuelo offers **Speak a question** again. Never ask a new question while Cuelo is still answering: continuous listening is not built.
